Design/Build/Fly

AIAA Design/Build/Fly is a national competition where teams design, build, and fly a radio-controlled aircraft to meet a specific mission profile.

Overview

As part of Ohio State's AIAA Design/Build/Fly team, my team and I designed, built, and tested a remote-controlled aircraft to meet the competition's mission requirements within a limited budget and timeline. Above is the finished aircraft after final assembly and paint, and pictured to the right is the mold of the plane shortly before the commencing of resin infusion.

Wing Construction

The wing's internal structure was built from laser-cut ribs and spars.

Composite Layup

My team applying a fiberglass and resin layup to one of the aircraft's structural components.

Custom-Fabricated Parts
