Design/Build/Fly

AIAA Design/Build/Fly is a national competition where teams design, build, and fly a radio-controlled aircraft to meet a specific mission profile.

Overview

As part of Ohio State's AIAA Design/Build/Fly team, my team and I designed, built, and tested a radio-controlled aircraft to meet the competition's mission requirements within a limited budget and timeline. Alongside is the finished aircraft after final assembly and paint.

Wing Construction

The wing's internal structure was built from laser-cut ribs and spars, then wrapped and vacuum-bagged to cure its composite skin.

Composite Layup

My team applying a fiberglass and resin layup to one of the aircraft's structural components.

Custom-Fabricated Parts

A custom payload housing I designed and dimensioned to fit the mission's specific requirements.
