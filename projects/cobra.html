Cobra Scientific

Cobra Scientific is a California-based government contractor specializing in defense and aerospace applications. I worked on non-destructive testing (NDT) software in MATLAB.

Unfortunately, due to NDA, I cannot display any pictures about what I did at Cobra in the summer of 2026. What I can say is that I programmed a computer-controlled motion system for ultrasonic scans and developed a method for remote access to the scan area using a camera system. All of this was for a novel nondestructive testing technique called Cryo-UT which is performed at low temperature. I also helped with assembling and fabricating prototype hardware systems.
